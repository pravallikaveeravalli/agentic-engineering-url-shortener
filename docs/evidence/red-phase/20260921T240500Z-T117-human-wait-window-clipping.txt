Fix: restored the maxOf/minOf clipping. Without it, a gate-wait interval that started before a recovery
window (e.g. left over from an earlier stage) would count its entire duration against this recovery's
human-wait figure, inflating the exclusion and deflating individualRecoveryDuration for a reason that has
nothing to do with this failure's own recovery.
